About SIVA

Two decades of passion, craft and legacy — cast in gold

Our Story

Born from Passion, Built on Trust

SIVA Gold & Silver Molding Works was founded in 2005 in the heart of Sankarankovil, Tamil Nadu, with a simple belief — every piece of jewellery should tell a story. What began as a small workshop driven by one master craftsman's vision has grown into one of the region's most trusted names in gold and silver jewellery.

For over two decades, we have dedicated ourselves to the art of traditional South Indian goldsmithing, blending age-old techniques with contemporary design sensibilities. Every ring, necklace, bangle and pendant that leaves our workshop carries the mark of hands that have poured generations of knowledge into their craft.

From bridal sets that grace the most important day of a family's life to everyday pieces worn with pride — we design your desires, one jewel at a time.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🏅
Purity First
Every gram of gold and silver we use is BIS hallmarked and certified. We never compromise on the quality of materials — your trust is our most precious metal.
🎨
Custom Craftsmanship
We believe jewellery is deeply personal. Every custom order is designed in close collaboration with our clients, ensuring the final piece is exactly what they envisioned.
🤝
Honest Pricing
Transparent rates, no hidden charges. We provide detailed weight certificates and making charge breakdowns with every piece — because trust is built in the details.
🌿
Heritage & Tradition
Our techniques are rooted in centuries of South Indian jewellery tradition — temple jewellery, antique finishes, Kundan work — preserved and passed on with pride.
⚡
Fast Turnaround
We respect your time. Most standard designs are delivered within 7–10 days. Bridal and custom bulk orders are scheduled with dedicated timelines.
🔒
Lifetime Support
Every piece we make is backed by our commitment. Free cleaning, minor repairs, and resizing support — because a relationship with our clients doesn't end at delivery.
How We Work

From Desire to Design

01
Consultation
Walk into our showroom or reach us on WhatsApp. Share your vision — the occasion, the design reference, your budget. Our team listens carefully and guides you on metal, weight and style.
02
Design & Quote
We sketch the design and share a detailed quote covering gold/silver weight, making charges and stone costs (if any). No surprises — everything is transparent before we begin.
03
Crafting
Our master artisans begin crafting your piece by hand using traditional methods. Each step — from wax casting to stone setting — is done with meticulous care.
04
Quality Check
Before delivery, every piece undergoes a thorough quality inspection — weight verification, finish check and purity testing. We only deliver what we are proud of.
05
Delivery
Your jewellery is handed over with a weight certificate and making charge receipt. We also offer safe home delivery within Sankarankovil and nearby areas.
