Our Story

Welcome to Luxurious Gold Design Molding

At SIVA Gold & Silver, every piece is more than jewellery — it is a legacy cast in gold. Founded in the heart of Sankarankovil, we have spent decades perfecting the art of traditional South Indian craftsmanship fused with contemporary design sensibilities.

Each creation passes through the hands of master artisans who pour generations of knowledge into every curve and setting, ensuring that what you wear is not merely an ornament but a story told in precious metal.
